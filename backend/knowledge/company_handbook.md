# Riverside Services: customer handbook

A stand-in document until the firm sends their own. It is written the way a real
one would be, in prose and headings rather than as a tidy list of questions and
answers, because that is the harder and more realistic case: the assistant has
to find the answer inside it rather than look one up.

Replace this file with the firm's own handbook, terms or policy document. The
answers change, nothing else does.

---

## Who we are

Riverside Services is a local firm covering plumbing, heating, electrical work,
cleaning, garden maintenance and vehicle servicing. We have been trading since
2011. All of our engineers are employed by us rather than subcontracted, and all
gas work is carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers.

We cover postcodes within roughly fifteen miles of the town centre. If you are
outside that we will say so when you book rather than after we have taken your
money.

## Hours and how quickly we can come

Our normal working hours are 8am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. The last appointment
of the day starts at 4pm so that a job can be finished before the engineer
leaves.

We do not work weekends as standard. Saturday morning appointments can sometimes
be arranged for an additional fifty pounds, subject to an engineer being
available.

For emergencies, meaning an active leak, a total loss of heating in winter, or
anything unsafe, we aim to attend the same day where the call comes in before
2pm. An emergency call out is charged at a higher rate, currently 140 pounds for
the first hour.

## What a visit costs

Every service has a price shown before you book. That price covers the first
hour on site, which is enough for most jobs.

Where a job runs longer, additional time is charged at 45 pounds per half hour.
The engineer will tell you before going over rather than presenting it
afterwards.

Parts are charged separately at cost plus fifteen per cent. The engineer will
tell you the price of a part before fitting it. If you would rather supply your
own parts we are happy to fit them, but we cannot guarantee parts we did not
supply.

There is no call out fee on top of the service price. The price you see is the
price of somebody arriving and starting work.

## Paying

We take card, bank transfer or cash. Payment is due on completion of the work
unless the job has been quoted and agreed otherwise.

For jobs above 500 pounds we ask for a twenty five per cent deposit at booking.
The deposit comes off the final bill and is refundable up to 48 hours before the
appointment.

We are not able to invoice private customers on account. Business and landlord
customers can apply for 30 day terms.

## Cancelling or moving an appointment

You can move or cancel an appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before it is
due to start.

Inside 24 hours we charge half the service price, because that slot can no
longer be offered to anybody else. If we have to cancel on you, which is rare,
you pay nothing and we will offer you the next available slot at no extra cost.

If nobody is home when the engineer arrives, and we have not heard from you, the
visit is charged in full.

## Guarantees

Labour is guaranteed for twelve months. If the same fault returns within that
time we will come back and put it right at no charge.

Parts carry the manufacturer's own guarantee, which is usually twelve or
twenty four months depending on the part.

The guarantee does not cover a different fault on the same appliance, damage
caused by somebody else working on it afterwards, or blockages caused by items
that should not have gone down a drain.

## Before the engineer arrives

Please make sure there is clear access to the area being worked on, and that
somebody over eighteen is at home for the whole visit.

If you know where your stopcock or fuse box is, it saves time to have that
information ready. If you do not, the engineer will find it.

We ask that pets are kept in another room while work is going on, for their
safety as much as the engineer's.

Parking needs to be available within a reasonable distance. Where parking is
paid or permit only, the cost is added to your bill.

## Complaints

If something is not right, tell us within 14 days and we will put it right.
Complaints go to the office rather than to the engineer, so that they are
recorded and followed up.

We aim to respond within two working days and to resolve a complaint within ten.

## Insurance and registration

We hold two million pounds of public liability insurance. Gas work is carried
out by Gas Safe registered engineers, and electrical work is carried out to
current wiring regulations by qualified electricians.

Certificates are issued for gas safety checks and electrical installation work,
and a copy is emailed to you within two working days of the visit.
