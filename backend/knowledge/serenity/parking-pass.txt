                                         TEMPORARY PARKING PASS REQUEST
All vehicles parked overnight in the guest parking area must be registered with the Association and approved by
Management. A temporary guest parking pass may be issued for a maximum of five (5) days per property, per calendar
month.

       A copy of the vehicle registration or rental agreement (if applicable) for the vehicle that will be temporarily parked on the property.

       A copy of the valid driver's license for the registered owner or tenant on record. A copy of the current lease agreement (tenants
        only).

Please Note: Temporary parking passes are subject to Association approval. Incomplete applications or missing
documentation may delay processing or result in the denial of the request. Vehicles parked overnight without a valid
temporary pass may be subject to towing or other enforcement actions in accordance with the Association's parking rules.
