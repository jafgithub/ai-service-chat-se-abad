MAILBOX & POST GUIDELINES

The replacement of a mailbox or post in the Three Lakes subdivision should consist of the
following materials and colors:

    •    The post must be of 4X4 construction, white plastic or cedar covered by white plastic of
        similar style as the figure detailed below. These posts are available at most local home
        building retailers such as Home Depot, Lowes, Menards or Ace Hardware.
    •    The mailbox must be of US Postal Service approved standard size, and be made of
        aluminum or plastic. The color should be white.
    •   You should install the mailbox with the bottom of the box at a vertical height of
        between 41‐45 inches from the road surface, unless you have a road or curb condition
        that prevents this. If you do, be sure to contact the postmaster before you change your
        mailbox location.
    •   Boxes must also be on the right‐hand side of the road and in the carrier’s direction of
        travel in all cases in which driving on the left‐hand side to reach the boxes would pose a
        traffic hazard or violate traffic laws and regulations.
    •   Your mailbox should be set back 26 inches from the front face of the curb or road edge
        to the mailbox door.

As always, if there is uncertainty, please contact the Design Review Committee for guidance.
