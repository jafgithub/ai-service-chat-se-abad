Additional Information and Instructions
    1.   The Design Review Committee (DRC) is established by the Declaration of Covenants and Restrictions (Covenants) of
         the Three Lakes Homeowners Association. The primary purpose of the DRC is to preserve and maintain the property
         values and natural beauty of Thee Lakes.
    2.   The Covenants and the associated Design Standards and Covenant Guidelines provide the basis for controlling
         changes in the yards and exterior appearance of the homes in the community. The controls provide for reasonable
         changes to all properties after review and approval by the Design Review Committee. The review process is designed
         to improve communication between homeowners, neighbors, DRC, and the HOA Board of Directors.
    3.   When you are considering making changes to your yard or the exterior of your home, please refer to the Design
         Standards and Covenant Guidelines to determine if a Design Review Form must be completed. You can also contact
         the Design Review Committee or Property Management Representative for guidance on whether the change must
         be submitted for review. If the change requires a submittal, you should complete this form by following the steps
         outlined on the previous page / front.
    4.   The Design Review Committee meets monthly. Any homeowner can attend the DRC meeting to voice an opinion or
         concern about a submittal.
    5.   The Design Review Committee will consider each Design Review Form based on the content of the submittal,
         comments from those attending or received in writing by the Homeowner Board, and the Design Standards and
         Covenant Guidelines. Each submittal will be judged on its own merit and either: approved as submitted, approved
         pending modifications, or disapproved.
    6.   The homeowner submitted the Design Review Form will be notified of the DRC decision in writing within thirty (30)
         days. Homeowners requesting notification of the decision by phone will be called as soon as possible after the
         decision.
    7.   Homeowners can appeal a decision of the Design Review Committee by contacting the Homeowners Board of
         Directors or the DRC Chairperson. The appeal will be presented to the Three Lakes Homeowners Association Board
         of Directors at the next scheduled Board meeting. The homeowner submitting the Design Review request can appeal
         up to 30 days after being notified of the decision. Other homeowners expressing concern before the decision can
         appeal up to ten (10) days after being notified of the decision.
