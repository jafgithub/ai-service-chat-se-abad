Architecture

The whole of it, on one page

One process, three engines, two places state lives. Everything a resident touches goes through the same API, and the only work done off this machine is phrasing, speech and email.

The embedding model runs inside this process, not as a service. That is why retrieval is fast, why a restart costs a few seconds of warm up, and why the memory limit on the unit is 1100M rather than something smaller.
